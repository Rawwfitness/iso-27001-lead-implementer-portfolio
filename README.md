ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Implementer Portfolio

About This Portfolio

This repository documents my learning journey in ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Implementer training through the Center for Professional Development at the International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA).

My objective is to develop practical skills in establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The portfolio will contain structured learning notes, practical exercises, risk assessment documentation, and implementation resources developed throughout the course.

Areas of Focus

- Information Security Management Systems (ISMS)
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements
- Information security risk assessment and treatment
- Statement of Applicability (SoA)
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 Annex A controls
- Information security policies and procedures
- Internal audit preparation and management review
- Corrective action and continual improvement
- ISMS implementation planning and documentation

Practical Projects

As I progress, I intend to develop practical exercises covering:

1. ISMS scope definition.
2. Information security risk identification and assessment.
3. Risk treatment planning.
4. Statement of Applicability development.
5. Annex A control implementation tracking.
6. Corrective action planning.
7. ISMS implementation roadmaps.

Standards Referenced

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Information security management systems: Requirements.
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 — Information security controls.
- ISO/IEC 27005:2022 — Guidance on managing information security risks.

Learning Objectives

My goal is to strengthen my ability to translate information security requirements into practical, risk-based management processes that support organizational resilience, accountability, and continual improvement.
