## 2. Purpose
 
This register identifies example information security risks affecting the fictional MediCloud Health Solutions environment.
 
The assessment illustrates a structured approach to risk identification, analysis, evaluation, and treatment planning.
 
The likelihood and impact scores below are illustrative and must not be interpreted as the results of a real security assessment.

## 5. Initial Risk Analysis
 
### R01 — Unauthorized Access to Patient Records
 
Unauthorized access could expose confidential patient information and undermine trust in the organization.
 
The illustrative score is 20 (Critical). Possible treatment measures include role-based access control, periodic access reviews, appropriate authentication, and access monitoring.
 
### R02 — Cloud Service Disruption
 
A cloud infrastructure failure could interrupt access to the healthcare platform.
 
The illustrative score is 15 (High). Possible treatment measures include resilience planning, recovery procedures, service-provider risk assessment, and regular recovery testing.
 
### R03 — Compromised Staff Accounts
 
Compromised credentials could allow an unauthorized person to access systems or information.
 
The illustrative score is 16 (High). Possible treatment measures include multifactor authentication, secure authentication practices, access reviews, and security awareness training.
 
### R04 — Loss of Patient Records
 
Insufficient backup arrangements or unsuccessful restoration could result in data loss and service disruption.
 
The illustrative score is 15 (High). Possible treatment measures include protected backups, restoration testing, and documented recovery procedures.
 
### R05 — Application Vulnerability
 
An exploitable application weakness could expose data or disrupt the service.
 
The illustrative score is 15 (High). Possible treatment measures include secure development practices, vulnerability management, security testing, and timely remediation.
 
## 6. Risk Treatment and Ownership
 
Each risk should be assigned to an appropriate risk owner.
 
The organization must evaluate its treatment options, select suitable controls, document treatment decisions, and determine how residual risks will be assessed and accepted by authorized personnel.
 
The possible treatment measures in this register are preliminary suggestions, not evidence that the controls have been implemented.
 
## 7. Review and Approval
 
This register is an educational example based on a fictional organization.
 
In a real implementation, the organization would validate its assets, threats, vulnerabilities, existing controls, scoring criteria, risk owners, and treatment decisions.
 
Risk assessment and treatment should follow the organization's approved methodology and the applicable requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
