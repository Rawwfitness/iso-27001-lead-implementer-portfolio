2. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the proposed scope of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) for MediCloud Health Solutions, a fictional healthcare technology organization.

The scope provides a clear boundary for identifying information security risks, establishing security controls, and managing the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

3. Organization Overview

MediCloud Health Solutions is a fictional healthcare technology provider that develops and operates a cloud-based platform for managing patient records and supporting healthcare service delivery.

The organization processes sensitive patient information and relies on cloud infrastructure, employees, healthcare partners, and third-party service providers.

4. Proposed ISMS Scope

The proposed ISMS covers the development, operation, maintenance, and support of the MediCloud patient management platform, including the personnel, information assets, supporting technology, and business processes directly involved in delivering the service.

The scope includes:

- Patient information and associated electronic records.
- Application development and maintenance.
- Cloud-hosted infrastructure supporting the platform.
- Identity and access management.
- Security monitoring and incident management.
- Backup and recovery processes.
- Personnel and third-party services supporting the platform.

5. Interested Parties

Relevant interested parties may include:

- Patients and authorized representatives.
- Healthcare organizations using the platform.
- Employees and contractors.
- Cloud service providers.
- Regulators and other applicable authorities.
- Business partners.

Their relevant information security requirements must be identified and evaluated during ISMS planning.

6. Dependencies and Interfaces

The proposed ISMS depends on cloud service providers, internet connectivity, third-party software, identity services, and healthcare partner integrations.

These dependencies should be evaluated during risk assessment and control planning.

7. Scope Exclusions

No exclusions have been finalized at this stage.

Any proposed exclusions or boundaries must be evaluated, justified, and documented in accordance with the applicable requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

8. Review and Approval

This document is a preliminary learning exercise. It has not been approved by an actual organization and does not represent a certified or operational ISMS.

A real implementation would require review and approval by appropriate organizational management.
