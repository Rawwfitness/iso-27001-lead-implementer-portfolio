## 2. Purpose
 
This document outlines proposed actions for treating the information security risks identified in the accompanying risk register.
 
The objective is to reduce information security risks to levels accepted by authorized organizational decision-makers, in accordance with the organization's risk treatment criteria.
 
All actions and timelines in this document are illustrative. They do not represent completed security activities.
 
## 3. Risk Treatment Approach
 
The organization will consider the following risk treatment options:
 
 
- **Risk modification:** Introduce or improve controls to reduce risk.
 
- **Risk avoidance:** Discontinue an activity that creates unacceptable risk.
 
- **Risk sharing:** Share some risk through appropriate contractual or other arrangements.
 
- **Risk retention:** Retain risk following an informed and authorized decision.
 

 
Risk treatment decisions must consider business requirements, applicable obligations, the effectiveness of available controls, and the organization's risk acceptance criteria.

## 5. Resource Requirements
 
Successful implementation may require:
 
 
- Appropriate personnel and technical expertise.
 
- Security tools and monitoring capabilities.
 
- Staff training and awareness activities.
 
- Backup and recovery resources.
 
- Vulnerability assessment and remediation processes.
 
- Management approval and budget allocation.
 

 
Resource requirements should be assessed and approved before implementation begins.
 
## 6. Risk Acceptance and Residual Risk
 
After treatment actions are implemented, each risk must be reassessed using the approved risk assessment methodology.
 
The organization should document:
 
 
- The controls implemented.
 
- Evidence supporting their effectiveness.
 
- The residual risk rating.
 
- Any remaining treatment actions.
 
- The decision of the authorized risk owner.
 

 
Residual risk must be accepted by the appropriate authority in accordance with the organization's established criteria.
 
No residual risk acceptance is implied by this draft.
 
## 7. Monitoring and Review
 
The responsible roles should monitor progress against approved targets.
 
Reviews should consider:
 
 
- Whether planned controls have been implemented.
 
- Whether the controls operate as intended.
 
- Whether risk levels have changed.
 
- Whether additional treatment is required.
 
- Whether relevant incidents or organizational changes require reassessment.
 

 
Updates should be documented and communicated to relevant stakeholders.
 
## 8. Relationship to the Statement of Applicability
 
The selected controls should be evaluated against the applicable ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A reference controls.
 
The organization must determine the necessary controls based on its risk treatment process and other applicable requirements, document its Statement of Applicability, and justify the inclusion or exclusion of Annex A reference controls as required by the standard.
 
The presence of a proposed control in this plan does not establish that it has been implemented or that the organization conforms to the standard.
