
Module 3: Context and Leadership

1. Top Management Responsibilities



Top management must demonstrate leadership and commitment to the Information Security Management System (ISMS).

Key responsibilities include:

Establishing the information security policy and objectives.

Integrating ISMS requirements into business processes.

Providing the resources needed to operate the ISMS.

Communicating the importance of effective information security management.


2. Information Security Policy — Clause 5.2



The Information Security Policy provides direction for managing information security within an organisation.

The policy must:

Be appropriate to the organisation's purpose.

Include information security objectives or provide a framework for setting them.

Commit to satisfying applicable information security requirements.

Commit to continual improvement of the ISMS.


The policy must be maintained as documented information, communicated within the organisation, and made available to interested parties where appropriate.

3. ISMS Scope and Interested Parties



An organisation must consider its business context and the requirements of relevant interested parties when defining its ISMS scope.

Excluding important systems or services without adequate justification may leave significant information security risks outside the ISMS.

4. Key Learning Points



Top management is responsible for establishing the Information Security Policy.

Effective ISMS implementation requires leadership, resources, and clear objectives.

The policy must support the organisation's purpose and applicable requirements.

ISMS scope decisions should consider the organisation's context and interested parties.


Conclusion

This module highlights the importance of leadership, the Information Security Policy, and a justified ISMS scope in establishing effective information security management.
