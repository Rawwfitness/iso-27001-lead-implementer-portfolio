Module 4: Planning and Risk Treatment

1. Statement of Applicability (SoA)

The Statement of Applicability documents the organisation's decisions about the information security controls relevant to its ISMS.

It records:

Whether each Annex A control is included or excluded.

The justification for including or excluding controls.

The implementation status of included controls.


The SoA supports transparency by explaining the organisation's control decisions and their relationship to information security risks.

2. Information Security Objectives — Clause 6.2

Information security objectives translate the organisation's information security policy into practical goals.

Objectives must be:

Consistent with the Information Security Policy.

Measurable where practicable.

Monitored.

Communicated.

Updated as appropriate.


Example: Achieve 100% completion of annual information security awareness training by the end of Q3.

3. Risk Treatment

Risk treatment involves selecting and implementing measures to address identified information security risks.

For example, implementing full-disk encryption on laptops reduces the likelihood that stolen devices will expose readable customer data.

This is an example of modifying or mitigating risk.

4. Key Learning Points

The SoA documents decisions about Annex A controls and provides justifications.

Control exclusions require documented justification.

Information security objectives should be clear and measurable where practicable.

Risk treatment decisions should address identified risks.

Progress toward security objectives should be monitored.


Conclusion

This module covers the role of the Statement of Applicability, information security objectives, and risk treatment in planning an effective ISMS.
