
# Module 5: Documented Information and Operation
 
## 1. Documented Information — Clause 7.5
 
Documented information must be properly managed throughout its lifecycle.
 
The organisation must ensure that documents are:
 
 
- **Identified:** Include suitable details such as title, date, author, and version.
 
- **Reviewed and approved:** Checked for suitability and adequacy before being issued.
 
- **Available:** Accessible where and when needed.
 
- **Protected:** Safeguarded against loss, unauthorised access, or unintended changes.
 
- **Controlled:** Managed throughout distribution, access, retrieval, storage, preservation, and disposal.
 

 
### Practical Example
 
When creating a new Incident Response Procedure, the organisation should review and approve it for suitability and adequacy before publishing it for staff use.
 
## 2. Operation — Clause 8
 
Clause 8 focuses on putting information security plans into practice.
 
### 2.1 Operational Planning and Control — Clause 8.1
 
The organisation must plan, implement, and control the processes required to meet information security requirements and carry out the actions identified during planning.
 
This includes:
 
 
- Implementing the Risk Treatment Plan.
 
- Putting selected information security controls into operation.
 
- Controlling planned changes.
 
- Reviewing unintended changes and taking action to reduce any negative effects.
 

 
### 2.2 Information Security Risk Assessment — Clause 8.2
 
Risk assessments must be conducted at planned intervals and when significant changes are proposed or occur.
 
Risk assessment should therefore be an ongoing activity, rather than a one-time exercise.
 
### 2.3 Information Security Risk Treatment — Clause 8.3
 
The organisation must implement its information security risk treatment plan.
 
This means carrying out the agreed actions to address identified information security risks.
 
### Practical Example
 
Before onboarding a new software vendor or carrying out a major IT infrastructure migration, an organisation can trigger a risk assessment to identify potential security issues before proceeding.
 
## 3. Awareness — Clause 7.3
 
Personnel must be aware of the Information Security Policy, their contribution to the effectiveness of the ISMS, and the implications of not conforming to ISMS requirements.
 
## 4. Key Learning Points
 
 
- Documented information must be reviewed, approved, available, protected, and controlled.
 
- Clause 8.1 covers operational planning and control.
 
- Clause 8.2 requires risk assessments at planned intervals and when significant changes occur or are proposed.
 
- Clause 8.3 requires implementation of the risk treatment plan.
 
- Clause 7.3 addresses awareness of information security requirements and the consequences of nonconformity.
 

 
## Conclusion
 
Effective ISMS implementation requires more than creating policies and plans. Documents must be controlled, security processes must be implemented, and risk assessments and treatment activities must continue throughout the organisation's operations.
