Module 1: Introduction to Information Security and ISO/IEC 27001

1. Introduction

Information security protects information and other valuable assets from unauthorized access, changes, loss, and disruption. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 provides requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

2. The CIA Triad

- Confidentiality: Ensures information is accessible only to authorized people.
- Integrity: Protects information from unauthorized or improper changes.
- Availability: Ensures authorized users can access information when needed.

3. ISO/IEC 27001:2022

The standard helps organizations manage information security risks and protect their information assets.

Its main management system clauses are:

- Clause 4: Context of the organization
- Clause 5: Leadership
- Clause 6: Planning
- Clause 7: Support
- Clause 8: Operation
- Clause 9: Performance evaluation
- Clause 10: Improvement

4. Lead Implementer vs. Lead Auditor

- Lead Implementer: Helps establish, implement, and improve an ISMS.
- Lead Auditor: Evaluates an ISMS to determine whether it meets the applicable requirements.
- Certification: The certification decision is made by the authorized certification body, not the Lead Implementer.

5. Key Learning Points

- Information security is a business responsibility, not just a technical task.
- Security controls should be based on identified risks.
- Effective implementation requires clear responsibilities, policies, planning, and continuous improvement.
- Risk assessment and risk treatment planning are addressed under Clause 6.

Conclusion

Module 1 introduced the fundamentals of information security, the CIA Triad, the structure of ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and the different responsibilities of Lead Implementers and Lead Auditors.
