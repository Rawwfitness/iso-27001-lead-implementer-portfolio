
Module 6: Annex A Controls — The 2022 Updates

1. Introduction to Annex A

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 contains 93 reference controls in Annex A, grouped into four categories. These controls help organisations select and manage information security measures according to their needs and risks.

The four categories also help organisations identify appropriate control owners across departments such as Human Resources, IT, and facilities management.

2. The Four Annex A Control Categories

2.1 Organisational Controls — 37 Controls

These controls address the organisational and administrative aspects of information security.

Examples:

- 5.1: Policies for information security.
- 5.19: Information security in supplier relationships.

2.2 People Controls — 8 Controls

These controls focus on the people involved in an organisation's activities, including employees and contractors.

Examples:

- 6.1: Screening.
- 6.6: Confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements.
- 6.7: Remote working.

2.3 Physical Controls — 14 Controls

These controls help protect buildings, equipment, and other physical assets from unauthorised access, damage, or loss.

Examples:

- 7.1: Physical security perimeters.
- 7.7: Clear desk and clear screen.
- 7.14: Secure disposal or re-use of equipment.

2.4 Technological Controls — 34 Controls

These controls address technical measures used to protect information systems, networks, applications, and data.

Examples:

- 8.5: Secure authentication.
- 8.12: Data leakage prevention.
- 8.24: Use of cryptography.

4. Practical Application

A Lead Implementer should consider which Annex A controls are appropriate for the organisation, based on its information security risks, requirements, and treatment decisions.

For example, an organisation that needs to prevent sensitive customer information from being sent outside the business without authorisation could consider Data Leakage Prevention (DLP) measures under control 8.12.

Control selection and implementation decisions should be documented in the Statement of Applicability (SoA), including the relevant justifications.

5. Key Learning Points

- Annex A contains 93 reference controls in ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- The controls are grouped into Organisational, People, Physical, and Technological categories.
- Each category addresses a different aspect of information security.
- Controls should be considered in relation to the organisation's risks and requirements.
- The SoA records control decisions and their justifications.

Conclusion

Understanding Annex A helps a Lead Implementer identify relevant information security controls, assign responsibilities, and support the implementation of an effective Information Security Management System (ISMS).
